Gem Seasons is a relaxing picture puzzle. Gems ride around a loop and drop into the spots of their own colour until the whole picture sparkles. There are 650 pictures in 14 worlds, plus holiday adventures that open for a short time each year.

Getting help

Found a problem or have a question? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed) and it will be answered as soon as possible.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a group of gems on the picture to lift them onto the loop. Each gem drops into an open spot of its own colour. Fill every spot to finish the picture. If the loop fills up with no place for any gem to go, the game tells you straight away.

The picture is too small to tap. Pinch to zoom, or use the + and − buttons.

What are the helpers? Extra loop spots give you more room on the loop, and the magic wand moves up to 10 gems straight into spots of their own colour. You can buy them with gems you earn by finishing pictures.

When are the holiday adventures? New Year, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day, Memorial Day, the 4th of July, Labor Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving and Christmas each open for a few days to a few weeks around the holiday, based on your device's date. You can play them again every year.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. If you leave in the middle of a puzzle, the game asks whether to continue or start over when you come back.

Can I turn off the sounds or vibration? Yes. There are switches for both on the home screen.

Does it cost anything? The game is free to play. Optional gem packs can be bought in the Store. There are no ads.
